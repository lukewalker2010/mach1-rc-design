# Wing Rib Profile Coordinates — CNC/Cutting Data

## Biconvex Airfoil Formula

```
y(x) = ±(t/2) × sin(π × x / c)
```

Where:
- x = chordwise position (0 = LE, c = TE)
- t = max thickness at that station = 0.035 × c
- c = local chord at that span station
- Upper surface: +y, Lower surface: −y

## Coordinate Convention

- Origin at LE of each rib
- x positive aft (toward TE)
- y positive upward
- All coordinates in **millimetres**
- 21 stations per surface (0% to 100% chord, 5% increments)

## CNC Cutting Notes

### For Rib Foam Cores (CNC hot-wire or 3-axis mill)
- Material: ROHACELL 71 HF, 8 mm sheet
- Cut upper and lower profiles separately, bond together
- Spar hole: drill 5.5 mm after bonding, at 30% chord
- Tolerance: ±0.1 mm on profile, ±0.2 mm on spar hole position

### For Rib Carbon Webs (CNC router or laser cut)
- Material: 0.5 mm carbon-epoxy sheet (cured layup)
- Cut outer profile matching biconvex shape
- Spar hole: 5.5 mm, aligned with foam core
- 3 mm flange on each face for skin bonding

### For DXF Export
- Export each rib as a closed polyline (upper LE → TE → lower TE → LE)
- Coordinate system: x = chordwise (0 at LE), y = thickness (0 at midplane)
- File naming: `wing_rib_R{n}_profile.dxf`
