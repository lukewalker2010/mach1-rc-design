## 2. Build Sequence

### Phase 1 — Tooling (40 hours estimated)

| Step | Task | Hours | Details |
|------|------|-------|---------|
| 1.1 | CNC mill fuselage plug from station table | 20 | PU foam block machined to fuselage station templates, sanded smooth |
| 1.2 | Splash mold from plug | 15 | Apply tooling gelcoat, 10 layers 200 g/m² fiberglass, cure, separate |
| 1.3 | CNC mill wing mold (female) | 5 | Two-cavity mold for upper and lower wing skins |
| **Phase 1 total** | | **40** | |

### Phase 2 — Structure (60 hours estimated)

| Step | Task | Hours | Details |
|------|------|-------|---------|
| 2.1 | Lay up fuselage halves | 10 | Hand layup of prepreg carbon in mold, vacuum bag, oven cure (120°C) |
| 2.2 | Trim and bond fuselage halves | 4 | Diamond cut off wheel, structural epoxy + 50 mm carbon doubler strips |
| 2.3 | Install bulkheads BH1–BH8 | 8 | Precision locate from station table, bond with flox structural fillets |
| 2.4 | Fabricate wing skins | 6 | Layup in mold, 4 layers 200 g/m² prepreg, vacuum bag, cure |
| 2.5 | Machine wing spar groove in foam core | 4 | CNC router pocket for 5 mm carbon rod, test fit |
| 2.6 | Insert spar + assemble wing | 2 | Bond carbon rod with structural epoxy, fit to foam core |
| 2.7 | Wing skin bonding | 8 | Bond upper and lower skins to foam core + spar, vacuum bag, cure |
| 2.8 | Trim wing to final shape | 2 | Diamond wheel, profile to airfoil template |
| 2.9 | Fabricate stabilator | 4 | Solid carbon laminate layup, cure, profile to NACA 64A-xxx, |
| 2.10 | Bond waist doubler + hardpoints | 4 | Internal doubler strips at wing root, M4 threaded inserts |
| 2.11 | Install engine mount ring and legs | 4 | 7075-T6 ring bonded to BH5/BH6, 3 legs with screws to engine |
| 2.12 | Bond belly skid brackets | 2 | 2 mm Al brackets to fuselage hardpoints |
| 2.13 | Install drogue chute container | 2 | G10 box bonded into tailcone, door hinged and fit-checked |

## 3. Safety Equipment Checklist

- [ ] **Fire extinguisher** — CO₂, 5 lb, at flight line (within 10 m of pit area)
- [ ] **Nomex flight gloves** — Required for engine starting and handling hot sections
- [ ] **Hearing protection** — 30+ dB NRR ear muffs (turbine = 120+ dB at close range)
- [ ] **Eye protection** — Safety glasses (impact rated, Z87+)
- [ ] **First aid kit** — At pit area
- [ ] **Fuel container** — Approved, grounded Jet-A1 container with spout
- [ ] **Fuel spill kit** — Absorbent pads, gloves, disposal bag
- [ ] **Fire blanket** — For engine fire containment
- [ ] **Grounding strap** — For fueling operation (static discharge prevention)
- [ ] **FAA COA or restricted airspace clearance** — Required for any high-speed / record flight
- [ ] **Insurance** — AMA membership + supplemental turbine waiver insurance
- [ ] **Doppler radar** — For speed verification (or certified GPS-logger for record)

## 4. Flight Day Checklist

### 4.1 Pre-Flight (2 hours prior)
- [ ] Weather check: wind < 10 kt, visibility > 5 km, no precipitation
- [ ] Airspace clearance confirmed (COA active, NOTAM issued if required)
- [ ] Flight area marked and spectator exclusion zone set (min 100 m radius)
- [ ] All safety equipment on-site (fire extinguisher, first aid, Nomex gloves)

### 4.2 Aircraft Preparation (1 hour prior)
- [ ] **Structural inspection:** All bond lines (fuselage seam, wing root, stabilator), no cracks or delamination
- [ ] **Fastener check:** All screws and bolts present and tight (M2 through M4)
- [ ] **Control surface:** Full deflection check — elevons, rudder if present — direction and range
- [ ] **Pushrod / ball link:** No slop, secure clevis retainers
- [ ] **Fuel system:** Tank full (1.5 L Jet A-1), no leaks, clunk free, vent clear
- [ ] **Battery:** 2S LiPo at storage voltage (7.6–7.8 V), charge if needed to 8.2 V
- [ ] **Telemetry / GPS:** Power on, satellite lock (min 10 sats), HDOP < 1.0
- [ ] **Receiver range check:** Reduced power mode, 100 m walk, no frame loss

### 4.3 Engine Start (30 min prior)
- [ ] **Fire extinguisher** positioned near tail, operator wearing Nomex gloves
- [ ] **ECU startup:** Connect ECU to computer (JetCat USB), verify firmware, parameters
- [ ] **Fuel prime:** Prime fuel line (pump in manual mode until fuel reaches engine)
- [ ] **Start:** Command start, monitor EGT/TIT (max 850°C on start)
- [ ] **Idle check:** 2 min at idle (60,000 RPM typical), verify stable EGT
- [ ] **Full-throttle run (5 s):** Spool to full (165,000 RPM), verify RPM, EGT, fuel flow
- [ ] **Return to idle:** Check idle stability after full-throttle

### 4.4 Launch Preparation (15 min prior)
- [ ] **Dolly attachment:** Place dolly under fuselage, engage latch pins, verify positive lock
- [ ] **Dolly release test:** On ground, cycle release servo, confirm clean separation
- [ ] **Drogue chute armed:** Door servo connected, safeties removed
- [ ] **Flight path clear:** Visual scan of airspace, observers in position
- [ ] **Go/no-go decision:** Wind, visibility, aircraft readiness, pilot readiness
- [ ] **Final control check:** Full deflection observed from behind aircraft

### 4.5 Launch Sequence
1. **Pilot:** "Cleared for takeoff" — advance throttle to full
2. Acceleration monitored by telemetry (GPS groundspeed)
3. At V_r ≈ 70 m/s (or positive pitch command from FC)
4. Dolly latch release (pitch-up detected by FC or Rx switch)
5. Clean separation confirmed visually
6. **Pilot:** "Positive rate, gear up" (dolly released)
7. Climb at 70 m/s to safe altitude (100 m AGL minimum)

### 4.6 In-Flight
- [ ] Trim aircraft at cruise speed (M0.2 for first flights)
- [ ] Monitor EGT, RPM, fuel level via telemetry
- [ ] Note any handling quirks (pitch sensitivity, roll rate, coupling)

### 4.7 Landing
- [ ] Throttle to idle, deploy drogue chute (if >15 m/s final)
- [ ] Belly skid contact, hold attitude until stop
- [ ] **Post-landing:** Engine shutdown (ECU spool-down cycle)
- [ ] Fuel remaining measurement, CG check for excursion data

### 4.8 Post-Flight
- [ ] Skid wear inspection, replace strips if worn below 2 mm
- [ ] Drogue chute repack (if deployed)
- [ ] Dolly inspection (if recovered), repair any damage
- [ ] Data download: GPS track, FC logs, ECU data
- [ ] Battery recharge (LiPo storage voltage)
- [ ] Debrief: handling notes, adjustments for next flight
